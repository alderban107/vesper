# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Discord users who want privacy without giving anything up. They live in servers, use voice channels daily, and expect everything to just work. They are not security researchers or sysadmins — they are gamers, friend groups, small communities, and hobby groups who have decided they're done with corporate surveillance but don't want a worse product as the trade-off. Self-hosters are a secondary audience: technically capable, motivated by ownership, but still expect the UI to get out of the way.

## Product Purpose

Vesper is a self-hosted, end-to-end encrypted messaging and voice platform that gives Discord-level UX with zero-knowledge privacy. MLS encryption is invisible — users never manage keys. Success looks like a Discord user switching to Vesper and noticing it feels more considered and premium, not noticing they switched away from a mass-market product.

## Brand Personality

Secure, calm, deliberate. Not austere — warm. The "evening star" name is right: something reliable that appears at dusk, not a floodlight. The product should feel like a well-made tool: no gimmicks, no noise, just the confidence that everything is handled.

## Anti-references

- **Signal / Telegram**: Utilitarian, institutional, looks like a security tool. Vesper should feel social, not clinical.
- **Generic dark-mode SaaS**: Purple gradient backgrounds, glowing cards, hero-metric layouts, identical feature grids. The aesthetic that screams "an AI made this landing page."
- **Discord itself**: Loud, cartoonish, saturated. Vesper targets Discord's users, not Discord's aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. **Privacy is invisible.** Encryption is never foregrounded in UI copy or visual chrome. It is the foundation, not the feature. The UI talks about conversations, not about keys.
2. **Discord-familiar, but considered.** Layouts should feel legible to someone coming from Discord, but every detail should feel like an upgrade — tighter spacing, more intentional color use, better hierarchy.
3. **Warmth earns trust.** The dark palette is not cold tech. The gold accent, soft violet, and warm off-whites push toward something people want to spend time in.
4. **Restraint over decoration.** When in doubt, remove. Vesper's confidence comes from what it doesn't do: no gradients for decoration, no glass-on-glass stacking, no motion for motion's sake.
5. **Community first.** Every screen is designed around the group, not the individual. Servers, channels, members — these are the protagonists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target, but the baseline expectation is that it works well for everyone. Minimum contrast ratios should be met in both dark and light themes. Reduced-motion preference should be respected for animations. Avoid conveying state through color alone.
